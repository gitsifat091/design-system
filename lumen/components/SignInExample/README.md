# SignInExample

An authentication screen: a brand panel and a focused form with large controls.

Rules it demonstrates:

- Auth and other touch-first forms use `size: 'lg'` controls (`control-lg`, 44px) and a full-width primary button.
- One column of fields, max 380px wide, `space-5` apart; title in `heading-2`.
- The error sits under the field it concerns and says what to do next.
- Secondary paths (SSO, reset, sign-up) are a secondary button and inline `link` text, never a second primary.
